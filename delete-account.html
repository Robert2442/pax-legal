ENGLISH - Delete Your Account

Warning: Deleting your account is permanent and cannot be undone. All your data including meditation history and preferences will be permanently deleted.

How to Delete Your Account

  1. Open the Pax app on your device
  2. Go to Settings (gear icon)
  3. Tap on "Account"
  4. Select "Delete Account"
  5. Confirm the deletion by following the prompts

Data Deleted

When you delete your account, we will delete: your profile, meditation records, preferences, and authentication data.

Subscription

If you have an active subscription, please cancel it through Google Play before deleting your account.

ESPANOL - Eliminar tu Cuenta

Advertencia: Eliminar tu cuenta es permanente e irreversible. Todos tus datos seran eliminados permanentemente.

Como Eliminar tu Cuenta

  1. Abre la app Pax en tu dispositivo
  2. Ve a Configuracion (icono de engranaje)
  3. Pulsa en "Cuenta"

Al eliminar tu cuenta, borraremos: tu perfil, registros de meditacion, preferencias y datos de autenticacion.
